The small Gemma 4 models are made for phones

The parts around Gemma

Three helpers hear you, write it down and speak

1 · hears

Silero VAD

An open-source, lightweight voice activity detection model. It labels each short chunk of audio as speech or not speech, in real time. Silence and background noise both count as not speech.

It tells the app when you start and stop talking.

2 · writes it down

Parakeet TDT 110M

NVIDIA’s open speech-to-text model, with 110 million parameters. It turns the audio of what you said into text.

That text is what Gemma reads.

3 · speaks

System voice

The text-to-speech voice built into the phone, the same one that reads your screen aloud. The app ships no voice of its own.

It speaks Gemma’s reply, one clause at a time.

The engine under Gemma

LiteRT-LM is what runs Gemma on the phone

what it is

Google’s open-source runtime for running language models on a device.

Gemma is the model file. LiteRT-LM is the program that loads it and does the math.

runs on

The phone’s GPU. It can use the CPU too, but starts slower.

gives back

The reply a piece at a time, so speech can start early.

where

Android, iPhone and iPad, from the same app code.

How it works · tool calling

Gemma only sees a tool when you ask for one

1 · you say

“Jot down that I want to try it Saturday.”

2 · the router

Save, action or chat?

A small embedding model (MiniLM) plus a tiny classifier, on the phone

Save

Gemma gets one tool: save_note

Everything else

Gemma gets no tools. It just talks.

Phone actions like timers or directions are planned. Today the router spots them and Gemma says it can’t do them yet.

Inside the router

One borrowed model reads the meaning. One tiny model I trained makes the call.

1 · reads the meaning

MiniLM

Text in, 384 numbers out. It cannot chat.

Similar meaning gives similar numbers.

22 million parameters, 90 MB. Used as downloaded.

2 · makes the call

The classifier

384 numbers in, three scores out: save, action, chat.

25,000 parameters, 258 KB. The only part I trained.

A tool is offered only when its score clearly beats chat.

trick 1 · the opening words

It reads the whole sentence and its first 3 words, then averages. The request is nearly always up front.

trick 2 · long messages

It also scores each clause on its own, so a request at the end of a story still fires.

What it could mean for the model in your pocket

Only open weights can be inspected and patched like this. A closed cloud model cannot.

The limit: a fix removes a failure mode. It does not add ability.
