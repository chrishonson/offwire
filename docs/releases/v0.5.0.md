# v0.5.0

Tagged 2026-11-28. Previous release: `v0.4.1`. The release evals were last run at `v0.3.0` (v0.4.0 and v0.4.1 did not change the AI), so the eval comparison below is with `v0.3.0`.

MINOR, because the router changed.

## What changed

- The router's encoder is all-MiniLM-L6-v2 fine-tuned on the router's training sentences (#15), not the stock model. R1 went from 270 to 284 of 304. Directions went from 11 to 13 of 17, and requests that got no tool from 20 to 10 of 179.
- R3, the holdout set, went from 59 to 71 of 72 on E4B and from 57 to 69 of 72 on E2B. Tool calls on no-tool turns there went from 3 of 30 to 0 on both.
- The router takes about 7 ms per turn on the Mac, down from 73 ms. The new model's tokenizer does not pad every message to 128 tokens.
- `router_eval.py` loads only the scorer configs a run uses. Since the fine-tuned model shipped, R2 and R3 had stopped at start-up.

## AI architecture at this tag

Unchanged from `v0.3.0` except for the router's encoder and its head. Models, prompts, tools and generation settings are as described in `docs/releases/v0.3.0.md`, `docs/releases/v0.2.0.md` and `docs/releases/v0.1.0.md`.

### Router

| Part | At this tag |
|---|---|
| Encoder | all-MiniLM-L6-v2 fine-tuned by `scripts/eval/finetune_router.py`: lr 3e-4 for 4 epochs, chosen out of fold over the 1,284-sentence training pool. `core/src/androidMain/assets/router/all-MiniLM-L6-v2-router.onnx`, 90,419,576 bytes, SHA-256 `d5c52622...12f7108`, fp32, ONNX Runtime 1.31.1 |
| Head | `embed-all-MiniLM-L6-v2-router-mlp.json`: one hidden layer of 64, head_words 3, l2 0.01, thresholds SAVE 1.0 and ACTION 1.3, split on |
| Routes | NONE has no tools. SAVE and SAVE+ACTION get save_note. ACTION gets the chat prompt with no tools, because the app has no do_action |

The stock model is the fine-tune's starting point and is no longer in the app. `scripts/fetch-router-model.sh` puts both models where the evals look.

What the numbers say:

- Nothing got worse. Gemma seeds 1-3 against 4-6 move R2 and R3 rows by 0 to 2 (`eval/studies/2026-11-27-router-quant`), and every row here is the same or better.
- The R3 gain is the router. On both models the same four holdout cases now pass: two directions requests ("How do I get to the hardware store from here?", "Navigate home"), the save "Remember, the recital is at 6:30 on Friday", and the over-call on "Log cabins are cozy, what's the best wood for them?". Nothing that passed at `v0.3.0` fails now. Still failing: `ho-put-a-note` on E4B and `ho-busy-afternoon` on E2B.
- The E2B answering-from-a-note row is still G10: all 3 runs of `recall-meetup` say "the twenty-fourth", which is right, and the scorer looks for "24".

The long-message evals R5 and R6 (open in #16) are not part of this release.
