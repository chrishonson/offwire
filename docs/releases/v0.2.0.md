# v0.2.0

Tagged 2026-11-14. Previous release: `v0.1.0`. The AI code is commit `0bc0a1b`. The tag adds the version number and these notes.

## What changed

- Gemma no longer saves a note when the user asks about notes, complains, or talks about notes. On the new save-decision set (O2), false saves went from 6 of 72 plus 3 parse errors to 0 of 72 on E4B, and from 9 of 72 to 0 of 72 on E2B.
- Asked whether it can save notes, Gemma says yes instead of denying it. Honest answers to capability questions went from 10 to 25 of 27 on E4B and from 15 to 24 of 27 on E2B.
- Save requests reach the tool more often. R2 save cases went from 21 to 27 of 27 on both models. O2 missed saves went from 3 of 48 to 0 on both.
- Every Android prompt says the app can save notes. A chat turn's prompt says this message does not ask for a save.
- The router was refit with 109 new training sentences: 54 NONE sentences about notes, complaints and corrections, and 55 save requests worded close to them.

## AI architecture at this tag

Unchanged from `v0.1.0` except for the router and the Android prompts below. Turn flow, models, tools and generation settings are as described in `docs/releases/v0.1.0.md`.

### Router

- Head: one hidden layer of 64 on the 384-dimension embedding, the whole message averaged with its first 3 words (4 at v0.1.0). Longer messages are also scored by sentence and clause.
- SAVE fires when its score beats NONE's by 1.0 (1.05 at v0.1.0). ACTION fires at 0.4 (0.5 at v0.1.0).
- Fitted with `embed_router.py fit --scorers mlp`, `l2=0.01`, on 1,035 sentences in `eval/router/train.jsonl` (926 at v0.1.0), plus `fit.json` and `routes.json`.

### Prompts

Built in `core/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/chrishonson/gemmavoice/llm/SystemInstruction.kt`. The intro, the live line, the limits line and the notes list are unchanged.

| Turn | Tool line |
|---|---|
| Android, route SAVE | "You have one tool, save_note. This app can save notes to the user's Notes screen on this phone. The user's message asks for what it does: call it once, before you say anything, then tell the user once, in a sentence, what you did. Never say you did anything the tool did not do." |
| Android, any other route | "This app can save notes to the user's Notes screen on this phone. The app gives you that tool only on a message that asks for a save. | iOS, every turn | Unchanged: "You have no tools. You cannot save notes." |

What the numbers say:

- The E2B R3 drop is 5 passes. Three are ACTION rows that the router now sends to chat, and ACTION measures a tool the app does not ship (G1). Two are the scorer reading "It all started with Google" as a claimed action.
- Chat turns are slower on the Mac because the new sentence pushes a prompt with no notes past a prefill step near 256 tokens. Prompts with a note or a turn of history were past it already.
- On the router's dev split, save requests sent to chat rose from 15 to 22 while the test split improved. That is the one mixed signal.

Result files: `eval/releases/v0.2.0/`. The O2 fixture is `eval/fixtures/save-decision.json`. The `tool_always` setup (`save_note` on every turn) was measured and not shipped: E4B saved on 11 of 87 R2 chat turns and E2B on 32 of 87.

## Known gaps

- G1: The Mac evals give ACTION turns a `do_action` tool the app does not have. In the app those turns are chat.
- G2: No eval runs with Thinking on.
- G5: Phone speed was measured only in the one session below.
- G6: iOS has no router and no tools.
- G7: The router sends some plain statements to SAVE. Gemma declined to save all three in the phone session, but each costs a conversation rebuild of about 8 s.
- G8: The first turn after the model loaded took 36 s on the Pixel. It was not measured at v0.1.0, so it is not known whether this is new.
