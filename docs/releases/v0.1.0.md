# v0.1.0

Tagged 2026-11-11. No previous release. This is the first stamped build, so the eval numbers here are the baseline that later releases are compared with.

## What is in it

- On Android, an embedding router reads each message first. Gemma then replies with one tool, `save_note`, or with no tools at all.
- The system prompt no longer makes Gemma refuse ordinary questions. On the Mac with E4B, refusals after a few turns went from 10 of 20 to 2 of 20, and it still declined 8 of 8 live or unsupported requests.
- The router sends requests for an idea, a name or advice to chat. 48 such requests were added to its training set.
- A Thinking switch in Settings, off by default.
- Barge-in keeps the conversation when a reply is cut off and the message is sent again.
- The classic chat list stays where the reader scrolled while a reply streams.
- The memory meter is full where the app meets the system's 3 GB share of RAM.

## AI architecture at this tag

### Turn flow

Android (`app-classic`, `app-console`):

1. Silero VAD cuts the microphone audio into utterances. Parakeet TDT 110M int8 (sherpa-onnx 2.0.1) transcribes each one. With the wake word on, only addressed speech goes further.
2. The router embeds the transcript with all-MiniLM-L6-v2 and scores SAVE, ACTION and NONE. Typed text takes the same path. Audio and photo prompts are not routed.
3. SAVE, or SAVE with ACTION: Gemma gets the routed prompt with `save_note`. Anything else: Gemma gets the routed prompt with no tools. The app has no phone-action tool, so an ACTION turn is plain chat and the prompt's limits line has Gemma say it cannot.
4. The reply streams to the screen and to speech. Text that follows a tool call is dropped once anything was spoken, and a repeated call with the same arguments does not run again (`ToolTurn`).
5. If the router fails to load or throws, the turn keeps `save_note` and the unrouted prompt.

iOS (`iosApp`): no router and no tools. Every turn uses the no-tools prompt.

### Models

| Role | Model | File and size | Runs on |

| Speech to text | Parakeet TDT 110M int8 | `assets/parakeet/`, in the APK | Android |
| Voice activity | Silero VAD | `assets/silero_vad.onnx` | Android |
| Speech output | Kyutai Pocket TTS int8, or the system voice | `sherpa-onnx-pocket-tts-int8-2026-01-26`, 98 MB, downloaded | Android |

### Router

- Head: one hidden layer of 64 on the 384-dimension embedding, the whole message averaged with its first 4 words. Longer messages are also scored by sentence and clause, and each route keeps its best score.
- SAVE fires when its score beats NONE's by 1.05. ACTION fires at 0.5. Nothing firing is NONE.
- Fitted with `embed_router.py fit --scorers mlp`, which chose `head_words=4` and `l2=0.01` by five-fold cross-fit on the training pool: 926 sentences in `eval/router/train.jsonl`, 104 in `fit.json` and 145 route examples in `routes.json`.
- Config: `core/src/androidMain/assets/router/embed-all-MiniLM-L6-v2-mlp.json`, the same file as `eval/router/embed-all-MiniLM-L6-v2-mlp.json`. `RouterParityTest` holds the Kotlin router to the Python one.

### Prompts and tools

All prompts are built in `core/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/chrishonson/gemmavoice/llm/SystemInstruction.kt`. Every prompt starts with the same intro and carries the same two lines:

> You cannot look anything up live, so you have no news, weather, scores or prices from today.

> You cannot set reminders, alarms or timers, call, text, email or message anyone, or book appointments. When asked, say plainly that you cannot. Never say you will search, check or get back to the user later.

| Turn | Tool line | Ends with |
|---|---|---|
| Android, route SAVE | "You have one tool, save_note. The user's message asks for what it does: call it once, before you say anything, then tell the user once, in a sentence, what you did." | The newest 40 notes, then "Answer everything else from what you learned in training: general knowledge, ideas, advice and conversation." |
| Android, any other route | "You have no tools in this reply. Never say you saved, noted, set, sent or added anything." | The same notes and closing line |
| iOS, every turn | "You have no tools. You cannot save notes." | "Answer from what you learned in training: general knowledge, ideas, advice and conversation." |

The one tool is `save_note`, defined in `core/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/chrishonson/gemmavoice/notes/SaveNote.kt`. No reply is fixed text. Gemma phrases every reply.

### Generation settings

- LiteRT-LM 0.19.1 on both platforms. Android runs Gemma on the GPU.
- Sampler: top-k 64, top-p 0.95, temperature 1.0. Context: 4,096 tokens.
- Thinking: off by default. When on, `ThinkingConfig(enableThinking = true)` is passed with each message and the reasoning is never shown or spoken.
- Tools are fixed per conversation. Chat stays on one conversation, and a save turn switches to a conversation with `save_note` and back.

What the numbers say:

- Every missed save is the router's. It sent 6 of 27 save rows to chat on the main set and 3 of 21 on the holdout set. When a save reached Gemma, the tool and the arguments were right every time.
- Every tool call on a no-tool turn in R3 started with the router labeling a plain message SAVE or ACTION. Gemma then called the tool on some of them.
- E2B answers a question from a saved note less reliably than E4B, 7 of 12 against 12 of 12.
- E4B says the confirmation before and after the tool call in 19 of 43 save turns. The app's guard drops the second one, so the person hears it once.
- The one R4 miss on E2B is an overheard fact it did not use on the next turn.
- The action rows are left out of this table. See G1.

## Known gaps

- G1: The Mac evals give the router's ACTION turns a `do_action` tool that the app does not have. ACTION rows above measure a tool that is not shipped. In the app those turns are chat.
- G2: No eval runs with Thinking on.
- G3: The refusal check that guided the prompt fix was a one-off script and is not in the repo. R4 covers limits and honesty, not refusals of ordinary questions after several turns.
- G4: The device gate ran after the tag, on the API 34 emulator (1080x2220), and failed on 4 of 399 tests. `core` passed 271 of 271 and `app-classic` 61 of 61. `app-console` failed 4 of 67: three in `SetupScreenUiTest` and `MainActivitySmokeTest.setupSurvivesActivityRecreate`. The Thinking row pushed the model rows and the microphone status below the fold of the Setup screen, and these tests assert they are displayed without scrolling to them. The screen scrolls, so this is a test defect and not an app defect. The tests were fixed on `main` after the tag and the gate then passed 399 of 399. The quality and iOS gates passed.
- G5: Speed on the phone was not measured for this release. The time to first token in the eval table is from the Mac.
- G6: iOS has no router and no tools.
