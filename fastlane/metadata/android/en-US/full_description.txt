Say "Computer" and ask. Offwire answers out loud in a calm, clear voice, and nothing you say ever leaves your phone.

HANDS-FREE
Open the app and talk. It listens for its name, answers, and keeps listening. Interrupt it mid-sentence and it stops and hears you out. No buttons to hold, no keyboard, nothing to read if you would rather not.

FULLY ON YOUR PHONE
Speech recognition, the language model and the voice all run on the device. Once the models are downloaded, Offwire works in airplane mode. There is no account, no sign-in, no tracking and no server on the other end.

A VOICE AND A LOOK FROM THE BRIDGE
Replies come in the measured voice of a starship computer, on a console styled after the classic LCARS displays: black panels, warm orange bars and large, high-contrast type.

MADE FOR EYES THAT TIRE
Everything on screen is large print with strong contrast, and the whole app follows your phone's font size setting. You can use it without looking at it at all.

A LIBRARY THAT STAYS HOME
Keep photos and notes in collections on the phone and ask about them by voice. The home inspection and medical record templates give structure without sending a single byte anywhere.

WHAT TO EXPECT
The assistant is a small model running on a phone. It is quick and private, and it can be wrong. It has no internet and cannot look things up, so treat its answers as a helpful first opinion, not a source of record.

REQUIREMENTS
Android 12 or newer, about 3 GB of free storage for the models, and a recent phone with 8 GB of memory or more for comfortable speed. Models download once over Wi-Fi.

OPEN SOURCE
Offwire is MIT licensed. The source, release notes and APKs are on GitHub.
